Installation
Three steps, you're live
📥
Step 01

Download the APK

Tap the download button. When Android asks, allow installs from unknown sources in your settings — it takes about 10 seconds and you only do it once.

✍️
Step 02

Install & sign up

Open the downloaded file, install AroundU, then create a free account with just an email and a username. No phone number, no credit card.

🌍
Step 03

Drop your first note

Tap the + button, pick a type — text, photo, GIF, voice, mood — and place it on the map. You're now part of the world.

Privacy first, always. We collect only your email, username, and the notes you create. Your data is never sold or handed to advertisers. You can delete any note — or your entire account — whenever you want.

Privacy Policy

Read before downloading · Last updated May 2026

By downloading and using AroundU, you agree to this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully.

1. What we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address (used for login only, never shown publicly)
  • Your display name and username
  • The notes, drawings, images, voice clips, and content you create
  • Location data you explicitly choose when pinning a note

2. How we use it

  • To run and maintain the AroundU service
  • To show your notes on the public map
  • To power the Friends feature
  • To send essential emails (password reset, etc.)

We do not use your data for advertising, tracking, or selling to third parties.

3. What's public

Your notes and display name are visible on the public map. Your email address is never shown to other users.

4. Deletion

Delete any note anytime from inside the app. To delete your account entirely, contact us at support@aroundu.app — all data is removed within 30 days.

5. Security

We use Firebase Auth and Firestore with industry-standard security rules to protect your data. No internet transmission is 100% secure, but we take this seriously.

6. Children

AroundU is not for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes means you accept the new terms.
